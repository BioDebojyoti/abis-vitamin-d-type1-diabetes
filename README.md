# abis-vitamin-d-type1-diabetes


We conducted a nested case–control study within a prospective birth cohort, randomly selecting 83 cases and 286 controls. Associations between 25-OH vitamin D, or 25-hydroxyvitamin D (also called calcifediol) levels in cord blood of children and disease risk were assessed. Two independent modeling methods were used:  

* Cox proportional hazards models to estimate hazard ratios (HRs) and 95% confidence intervals (CIs).
* Condition regrssion models with covariate balancing (using [WeightIt](https://ngreifer.github.io/WeightIt/) ) to estimate odds ratios (ORs) and 95% confidence intervals (CIs).
